## DATA DESCRIPTION

Stream-sediment samples form a principal sampling medium of the
National Geochemical Mapping (NGCM) programme.

GSI documentation describes systematic stream-sediment sampling at
approximately 2 km × 2 km unit-cell scale for regional geochemical
mapping.

The NGCM geochemical database is intended to characterize regional
elemental distributions and support applications including mineral
resource targeting and other geoscientific/environmental studies.

GSI documentation indicates analysis for up to 66 elements.
